# X. Limit breaker: every limit the research hit, and how to break or bend it without losing depth

Stance taken: "every limit is a hypothesis until it is measured; when it is real, look for the side door."
Inputs: CONTEXT, V3, T, A1–A6, R6–R10, J1–J6, RULES_v2/v3, round 1 (A–D, R1–R5, V2), `docs/BUILD_GUIDE.md` §3, CLAUDE.md (refusal history 28.10).
Tags: **[R]** real and measured/documented, **[A]** assumed, **[P]** only the pilot can tell. Gains are on the R8/R10 calendar (everything at 8 + 1 h: R8 P50 17.6–18 months; R10 tiered 27.8; core 21.1). Hours base ≈ 21,100 effective pages × 7.0 all-in min/page ≈ 2,460 h (R8), ≈ 3,070 h with R10 tiers + safeguards.

## 0. The one-line finding

Only two resources are scarce: **his fresh-state hours (≈ 6 EFH/day at 8 h, R9)** and **his retention**. Everything else (compute, latency, input caps, refusals, grading, internet, scheduling) is a compute-side limit and can be broken by moving work to the night factory. The limit-breakers that matter therefore all do one of three things: (a) cut **minutes per source page** without cutting a depth unit, (b) raise the **share of the day that is useful** at lower load, (c) stop **leaks** (waiting, disputes, re-learning, rebuilding). No combination found makes "a few months" true for both apps at full depth; the best honest combination takes ~25–30% off the hours, and the pilot decides whether it is 25 or 35.

## 2. Ideas checked and rejected or capped (honesty)

- **"A simulation carries 10 depth units in 2 minutes."** Capped. A widget delivers *structure* and *magnitude* units (D1) fast, but the J-judges count origin/assumption-entry and critique as units, and no widget delivers them. Counted only as replacing the sweep narrative and one think box. Must be predict-first (commit, then slide), otherwise it is passive watching.
- **Speed reading on mastered material** without a test: rejected. Only collapse after a passed closed-book probe.
- **Cheaper model writes lessons, Opus checks:** rejected for core lessons (his preference, no J-evidence). Allowed for lint, number extraction and second grading.
- **10+ h days:** R9: ≈ 0 extra EFH by month 3–4. Not a breaker.
- **Audio lessons as first exposure:** rejected as D1; allowed as pre-listen (priming) and walk review.

## 4. How they combine

A day: **night** — factory writes and verifies tomorrow's 3–4 dense lessons, grades yesterday's free text with two models, rebuilds the course memory from the ledger, regenerates the review queue, fetches cited current examples, and prepares 5 pre-questions. **Morning** — closed-book rebuild of yesterday's last lesson (sleep relearning), then 4–5 h dense first-exposure lessons, each opening with a test-out probe that may collapse it. **Afternoon** — nap, forge/project work with entry tickets (counts as review), delta/transfer tests. **Walk** — teach-back and Fermi drills. **Evening** — light notebook writing, pre-questions for tomorrow.

Combined, the leak-stoppers and depth-neutral bends compound to roughly **−25 to −30% hours on R10's tiered plan**: everything ≈ 27.8 → **~20 months** at 8 + 1, core ≈ 21.1 → **~15 months** (P50; the pilot band is wide). If the pilot's minutes per page land near 5 rather than 7, core lands near **11–12 months** and everything near 15. **"A few months" for both at full depth remains out of reach**; the way to honour the stance is to make the first authority outputs (flagship projects, cleared articles) arrive by month 3–4 while the curriculum runs, so the authority goal is not hostage to the last page.

Everything above stays depth-neutral only under L14: if the external benchmark shows a delayed-retest drop, the breaker that caused it is rolled back.
