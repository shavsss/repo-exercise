# MG: a crosswalk between the books and the body-of-knowledge (BoK) maps, and one unified topic map

## How to rebuild

Run each script with `python3 -I`, in this order:

1. `MG_match.py` builds the corpus: 6,040 units (Gilua 4,492 sections and Lanchester 1,548 units). Each unit has a title field and its full book text. It then runs TF-IDF cosine (unigrams + bigrams, crude stemming) against 1,353 BoK subtopics. Each subtopic is queried twice: by its full name, and by its head phrase (the text before the first `:`, `,`, `(`, "vs" or "and").
2. `MG_rerank.py` multiplies each score by the IDF-weighted share of the subtopic's own terms found in the section, times 0.6 for non-teaching section types. A match on the head phrase alone is discounted by 0.8.
3. `MG_build.py` produces the coverage labels, the crosswalk between sections and subtopics, the cross-app unification, the unified map and the totals.
4. `MG_blocks.py` lists the biggest blocks of pages that are not needed.
5. `MG_sample.py` and `MG_recall.py` support the hand checks.

The intermediates (`MG_scores.json`, `MG_rr.json`, `MG_crosswalk.json`, `MG_bodies.pkl`) are regenerated by the scripts. They were deleted to keep this folder within its brief.

**Output:** `MG_topics.json` has these keys:

| key | contents |
|---|---|
| `domains` | domain → topic → subtopic. Each subtopic has: importance, coverage, best score, modules, a host for gaps, up to 6 sections (key, score, pages, title) and estimated items (cr/K/S/C/pages). Lanchester items also carry `delta`, `unified_with`, `test_out`, `op_bg` and `lab_part`. Gilua items carry `military_twins`. |
| `crosswalk_sections` | every section and unit: its class, type, pages, PDF pages, modules, mapped subtopics and inherited importance |
| `gaps` | the K/S gaps |
| `unified_pairs` | the cross-app pairs |

## Thresholds and precision (hand-read: 96 pairs plus 24 recall checks)

Each sampled pair was the top section for a random subtopic, read against the section text.

| final score | label | precision |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 0.20 | full | **31/32 = 0.97** |
| 0.13–0.20 | partial | **16/32 = 0.50** (first pass 11/16, second pass after the head queries 5/16) |
| 0.09–0.13 | weak (counted as missing) | ≈ 0.28 (4.5/16) |
| < 0.09 | missing | ≈ 0.16 (2.5/16) |

- **The "missing" label itself was checked on 24 random gaps: about 0.69 are truly absent from the books.** The false misses are:
  - SMAD content (image-only, e.g. earth-central angle);
  - items in K1 and K3 (improper integrals, operators);
  - items in E17 (learned perception).
- SMAD units have no text. A title hit ≥ 0.09 on a SMAD unit is counted as partial.
- **Caveat on scope:** Lanchester articles are outside the registry, so some Lanchester "gaps" (Link 16, kill webs, doctrine items) may be covered by articles.
- **Caveat on section types:** the type classifier has 0.94 accuracy on teaching vs non-teaching. For example, E22 ch. 4 "Overview of Pipelining" was typed as history.

**Conservative rules applied:**
- Importance rounds up: the section inherits the maximum importance among its mapped subtopics.
- A section is N-only only when the N match scores ≥ 0.20.
- Military history is classed as test-out, not removal.
- Unmapped teaching sections are kept.
